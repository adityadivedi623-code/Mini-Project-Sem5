BEFORE YOU BOOK

A few useful answers.

How often should I service my AC?

Check your manufacturer’s maintenance guidance. Usage, dust and operating conditions determine the right schedule. Clean accessible filters as directed and arrange a professional inspection when performance changes.

My AC is running but not cooling. What should I do?

Check the temperature setting and accessible filters. If the problem continues, switch it off and request an inspection. Low cooling does not automatically mean a gas refill is needed.

Are parts and refrigerant included in the service?

A cleaning visit and a repair are different jobs. Any parts, refrigerant or additional work should be listed in an estimate and approved before work begins.

Can I book and pay online?

This website saves your inquiry on the company server. It does not accept payments or confirm appointments. A service provider must confirm availability and the final quote.

Which AC capacity should I choose?

Room area alone is not enough. Ceiling height, sunlight, occupants, appliances and insulation also matter. Ask for a heat-load assessment before selecting capacity.
